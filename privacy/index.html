Privacy policy

The short version: your health data stays on your iPhone. Haa doesn't send it to us or anyone else, and never uses it for ads.

Who we are

Haa is made by Tap Get. If you have a question about your data, email info@tapgetit.com.

What Haa reads

  • Heart rate from Apple Health during and around a game, and your resting heart rate, with your permission. Or live heart rate from a Bluetooth wearable you connect.
  • Your approximate location, once, to start your swim on the map from where you are, with your permission.

What Haa writes

  • Your sessions, as mindful minutes in Apple Health.
  • During a game on Apple Watch, Haa runs a short mind and body workout so the watch reads your heart rate live. It isn't saved to Apple Health.

Where your data stays

Your health data, your location and your session history stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Haa has no account and no server of its own. We don't receive your health data, and Haa never sells it, shares it for advertising or uses it to choose what you see.

Services Haa uses

  • Apple. The App Store handles payment. Apple Health stores what Haa reads and writes. Searching for a place on the map uses Apple Maps.
  • RevenueCat manages Haa Plus subscriptions. It receives an anonymous app ID and your purchase history from the App Store, nothing about your health. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Ads

Haa doesn't show ads from ad networks and has no tracking code. If that ever changes, this page will say so first.

Your choices

  • Change what Haa may read or write in Settings, Health, Data Access and Devices, Haa.
  • Turn off location in Settings, Privacy and Security, Location Services, Haa.
  • Deleting Haa deletes everything it kept on your iPhone. What it wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Children

Haa is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with its date.
