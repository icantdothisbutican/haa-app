Help and contact

Haa is a breathing game you play with your heart rate. Breathe slowly along the line, keep your heart rate under the goal, and the shark stays behind you.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email info@tapgetit.com.

Connecting your heart rate

  • Apple Watch: install Haa on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone, wear it snugly, and keep it unlocked. Starting a game opens Haa on the watch; it can take up to 30 seconds the first time.
  • Other wearables: Whoop, Garmin, Polar, Coros and most chest straps work when they broadcast heart rate over Bluetooth. Turn on heart rate broadcast on the device, then connect it under You, Heart rate, Other wearable.
  • No watch? Every game can be played by breath alone.

If the watch doesn't connect

  • Open Haa on the watch once yourself, then try again from the iPhone.
  • Check that Haa may read your heart rate: Settings, Health, Data Access and Devices, Haa.
  • Restart the watch if it still won't connect.

Haa Plus

Haa Plus unlocks every game, every length, endless mode and no ads. It's a subscription through the App Store and renews automatically. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews in Settings on your iPhone, under your name, Subscriptions. To get Haa Plus back on a new phone, tap Restore purchases under You in Haa.

Your data

Your health data stays on your iPhone. Haa never sells it or uses it for ads. Read the privacy policy.

Haa is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.
